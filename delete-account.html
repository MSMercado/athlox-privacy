Delete Your AthloX Account

Developer: Steven Mercado · App: AthloX

If you would like to delete your AthloX account and all of your associated data, you can request deletion by following the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to stevenmercado83@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: AthloX Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address associated with your AthloX account in the message body
  4. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 30 days
  5. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted
A self-service in-app "Delete my account" button is planned in a future update. In the meantime, email-based requests are processed manually and promptly.

Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account (email, name, user ID)
  • Profile information (weight, height, fitness goals, onboarding preferences)
  • Workout plans and workout history (sets, reps, logged exercises)
  • GPS run history and associated location data
  • Community posts, photos and any content you have shared in the app
  • AI Coach conversation history

Data that may be retained

  • Anonymised, aggregated analytics (e.g. total workout counts) may be retained indefinitely as they cannot be linked back to your identity.
  • Records required for legal or security purposes (e.g. abuse reports, fraud prevention logs) may be retained for up to 90 days after deletion.
  • Encrypted backups may contain a copy of your data for up to 30 days before they roll over and are permanently overwritten.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or how your data is handled, please see our Privacy Policy or email stevenmercado83@gmail.com.
