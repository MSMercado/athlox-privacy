AthloX — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 19 April 2026  ·  Last Updated: 19 April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AthloX (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and shares your personal information when you use the AthloX mobile application (the “App”).

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about the data we handle. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely as a hashed value by our authentication provider)

1.2 Profile & Fitness Data

  • Height, weight, age, sex, fitness goals, and experience level
  • Workout plans, completed workouts, exercise logs, and progress data
  • Profile photo (optional, uploaded by you)

1.3 Content You Create

  • Posts, photos, captions, and other content you share in the community feed
  • Messages you send to the in-app AI Coach

1.4 Device & Permission Data

  • Camera and photo library access (only when you explicitly choose to add a photo)
  • Location data (only when you explicitly enable workout tracking that requires it)
  • Push notification tokens (only if you enable notifications)

1.5 Technical & Usage Data

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Crash reports and basic usage analytics (used to improve stability)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your AthloX account
  • To generate personalised workout plans and AI coaching responses
  • To display your posts and profile in the community feed
  • To send notifications you have opted into
  • To improve, troubleshoot, and secure the App
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

AthloX uses the following third-party services to provide core functionality. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data we send.

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage, and serverless functions. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Groq — large language model inference for workout plan generation and the AI Coach. Messages you send to the AI Coach and prompts used to generate plans are sent to Groq for processing. See groq.com/privacy-policy.
  • Anthropic (where used) — additional AI coaching features. See anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Expo — application framework and over-the-air update infrastructure. See expo.dev/privacy.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers operated by Supabase, with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. We follow industry-standard security practices, but no system is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your account password confidential.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information, posts, and uploaded photos will be permanently removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability — request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent — for any processing based on consent
  • Object or restrict processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority
To exercise any of these rights, email athloxadmin@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Region-Specific Notes

  • European Economic Area / United Kingdom (GDPR): Our legal bases for processing are consent, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, and legal obligation, depending on the activity.
  • Australia (Privacy Act 1988 & Australian Privacy Principles): You may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au if you believe we have mishandled your data.
  • Philippines (Data Privacy Act of 2012, RA 10173): You may contact the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.
  • California (CCPA / CPRA): California residents have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

7. Children's Privacy

AthloX is not intended for users under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at athloxadmin@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than the one in which you reside. By using the App, you consent to such transfers. We rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) where required by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by email. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
